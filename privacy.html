Privacy policy

Juicy Block · last updated 4 October 2026

Juicy Block does not collect any personal data.

What the app stores

Your best scores, settings, Fruit Goals, Daily streak and the run in progress are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What the app does not do

Juicy Block has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the internet, your location, your contacts, your camera, your microphone or your photos.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a Daily result, iOS opens the standard share sheet with a short text such as your score. You choose where it goes; the app itself sends nothing.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Apple

Apple may collect information when you download apps from the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive that data.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this (for example by adding advertising), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will change too.

Contact

Developer: Mustapha Mokrani, Germany. Questions about privacy: see the support page.
