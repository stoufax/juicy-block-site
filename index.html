Juicy Block

Fruit block puzzle for iPhone

Drag fruit blocks onto the board, fill rows and columns, slice the fruit and pour the juice into your glass. Fill the glass to brew a fruit mocktail and start a Juice Rush.

Play Endless, run through Rounds with powers, or take on the Daily challenge with a new twist every day. Juicy Block works fully offline.
